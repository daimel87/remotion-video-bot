# Animal Facts Reactions — YouTube Shorts titles

When asked for a video title for this channel, use this format:

- Short and punchy (fits without truncating in the Shorts feed / Studio list).
- One clear hook, no long subordinate clauses.
- End with a single relevant emoji.

Example: "This Beetle Shoots Boiling Chemicals! 🔥"

Longer, paragraph-style titles get truncated in the feed and in YouTube Studio's
content list — avoid them.
